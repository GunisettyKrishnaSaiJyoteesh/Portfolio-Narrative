Case Study 01 — Crowd Intelligence

Teaching cameras
to count a crowd.

The problem

Crowds turn dangerous precisely when they become too dense to count. By the time a human notices, it's late.

The build

A CSRNet deep network reads live video through OpenCV and renders a density map — an estimate of how many people occupy every region of the frame, in real time.

The payoff

A Streamlit control room watches the numbers so nobody has to: when a zone crosses its threshold, an automated overcrowding alert goes out by email.

Case Study 02 — Medical Imaging

A second pair of eyes
for the radiologist.

The problem

A chest X-ray holds subtle abnormalities, and reading them at volume is exhausting, high-stakes work.

The build

YOLOv8 on PyTorch, trained on VinDr-CXR DICOM images, detects and localises lung abnormalities — drawing a box around what it sees, not just naming it.

The payoff

Grad-CAM overlays show why the model flagged a region, turning a black box into something a clinician can interrogate and trust.

Case Study 03 — Market Segmentation

Four markets were hiding
inside one.

The problem

An EV manufacturer entering India doesn't face one market — it faces several, wearing the same disguise. Guess wrong and the launch misses everyone.

The build

Live EV datasets pulled through REST APIs, cleaned and clustered in Python — part of a body of segmentation work spanning 50,000+ records at Feynn Labs.

The payoff

Distinct customer segments with clear profiles, translated into concrete entry strategies a manufacturer could act on.
